I am a Research Associate in Statistics at the University of Cambridge.

My research is about developing statistical methods for complex data, with a focus on network analysis, Bayesian inference, and density-based clustering. I like problems that sit at the edge of what theory can currently explain.

I also teach and try to make statistics feel a little less intimidating.

Research

I develop statistical methods for structured and relational data, motivated by problems where the underlying network or clustering structure matters — both theoretically and in practice.

Interests

Network data

Statistical models and inference for graph-structured data — stochastic block models, latent position models, and extensions to dynamic and multilayer settings.

Clustering and community detection

Algorithmic and theoretical aspects of recovering latent group structure, including spectral methods and regularisation under sparsity.

Bayesian statistics

Nonparametric and hierarchical Bayesian models for complex structured data, with a focus on scalable inference and interpretability.
